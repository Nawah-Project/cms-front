## Organized entry

In the experiment editor, select Home and “Organized entry (A then B)”. This is a 50/50 alternating enrollment mode, with Task-zero completion as its durable common outcome. The first eligible visit assigns A, the next assigns B, globally. New accounts and existing accounts still at Task zero without group membership are eligible. Advanced accounts, administrators and the dedicated production QA identity are excluded. The experiment's monotonic database counter and account assignment are transactionally saved; repeated logins, multiple tabs, concurrent requests and account deletion cannot redraw or rewind the arm.

Enrollment begins only for a RUNNING experiment. Pausing restores the section release while retaining arms for resume. Completing an experiment preserves evidence and membership cohorts. The report uses all eligible assigned accounts, rather than home views (A has no home screen), and checks actual completed Task-zero state. It is descriptive; sample planning does not prove statistical significance. The reporting window filters assignment time, with current membership used by the group filter.
